Dynamic Glyph for Ingress

Gear drops and hack statistics

Your gear, counted

After each hack, the app reads the item panels on your screen and counts the gear shown, by item, level and rarity.

Any period, in numbers

Pick a day, 7 days or 30 days, or drag the timeline to any period you like. Statistics shows your hacks and gear for it, by hack length and by command. Export your gear readings as CSV when you want the raw numbers.

Revisit saved hacks

Saved hacks include bonuses, the Glyphs the game accepted, portal level and gear readings. Results are kept for up to 90 days, with limits of 1,000 hacks and 1,000 drops.

On your Home Screen

The Home Screen widget shows your totals for Today, the last 7 days or All, with daily averages, in three sizes. Start and stop from Control Center, or use Shortcuts and Siri to start, stop or open Statistics.

Glyphs, if you want them

The app can also keep the Glyph sequence in view while you draw: the next Glyph in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, the whole sequence in a floating window over the game. With your own sequence list imported, it can predict upcoming Glyphs before the game shows them. Predictions can be wrong.

Only counting gear? That's how it starts: gear is counted from your first hack, with no list needed.

Your data stays yours

Screen processing happens on your iPhone, with the system recording indicator visible throughout. No account, no server, no analytics. Optional iCloud sync, off until you turn it on, sends saved results and gear readings to your private iCloud. You control any exports and reports you choose to share.

Before you install it

Dynamic Glyph is unofficial software, made by a player, with no connection to Niantic Spatial, who run Ingress. Their Player Guidelines list “using modified or unofficial software” as cheating. This app does not modify the game and never talks to it: it reads your own screen, on your own phone. The guidelines draw no line between software that changes the game and software that helps you play it.

A violation can lead to a warning, degraded gameplay, suspension or permanent termination, sometimes without warning, and can reach every Niantic Spatial product. That risk is yours.

Read the Player Guidelines and the Terms of Service.

What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
  • Ingress, installed and signed in. It is a separate app and is not included.
  • For Glyph assistance, your own Glyph list file; for predictions, your own sequence list too. Counting gear needs neither.
