Dynamic Glyph

The Glyph sequence, in view while you draw.

Dynamic Glyph reads the hack from your own screen and keeps the shapes in front of you — in a floating window over the game, and in the Dynamic Island. Recognition runs on the iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.

What it does

The sequence, as it appears

A floating window stays on top of the game and shows the whole sequence, with the shape to draw now outlined.

In the Dynamic Island

The Glyph to draw now and the one after it, on the Island and the Lock Screen.

Every hack counted

Results and the items each hack drops are kept in Statistics, with charts and a CSV export.

Nothing leaves the phone

No account, no server, no analytics. Reading the screen happens on the device, and the recording indicator stays visible throughout.

Before you install it

Dynamic Glyph is unofficial software, made by a player, with no connection to Niantic Spatial, who run Ingress. Their Player Guidelines list “using modified or unofficial software” as cheating. This app does not modify the game and never talks to it: it reads your own screen, on your own phone. The guidelines draw no line between software that changes the game and software that helps you play it.

A violation can lead to a warning, degraded gameplay, suspension or permanent termination, sometimes without warning, and can reach every Niantic Spatial product. That risk is yours.

Read the Player Guidelines and the Terms of Service.
