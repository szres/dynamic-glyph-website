Dynamic Glyph

Support

Dynamic Glyph is an iPhone app that reads an Ingress Glyph hack from your own screen and keeps the sequence in view while you draw.

Get in touch

Email dynamic-glyph@szres.org

Telegram t.me/Dynamic_Glyph

Expect a reply within three business days. Writing from the app’s Settings screen, under Get support, fills in the address for you.

Common questions

The Start button is greyed out.
Glyph mode needs your own shape list file (glyph_line.csv) before it can start. Add it from the card on the Home screen or in Settings, once. Counting gear drops needs no file: switch Glyph assistance off in Settings.
Nothing shows in the Dynamic Island.
Live Activities must be on for the app: open the iPhone’s Settings app, find Dynamic Glyph, and turn them on. On iOS 18 to 26 the Island cannot be used on its own — the floating window has to stay open for the app to keep reading.
The floating window is in the way.
Pinch it to resize, drag it to move it, or tuck it against the side of the screen. Keep it clear of the row of hexagons at the top of the game: that is where the app reads the sequence from.
Does it work without Ingress?
No. The app reads what the game draws on your screen, so Ingress has to be installed and running. Dynamic Glyph never connects to the game or to any server.
Where are my results, and how do I delete them?
In Statistics. The app keeps up to 1,000 hacks and 1,000 item drops for 90 days, on the phone. Clear results, in the result options sheet, deletes them; individual rows can be deleted from the Select mode. Export writes a CSV you can keep.
The item counts look wrong.
Reading gear drops is marked as a test feature: some items can be missed or misread. From a drop you can send a report, which attaches the reading and its picture so the misread can be fixed.

What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
  • Ingress, installed and signed in. It is a separate app and is not included.
  • For Glyph mode, your own shape list file. For gear drops only, nothing extra.
  • The Dynamic Island display needs an iPhone that has one, with Live Activities on.
