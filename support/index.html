Support

Dynamic Glyph reads a Glyph hack from your own iPhone screen and keeps the sequence in view while you draw.

Email: dynamic-glyph@szres.org

Telegram: @Dynamic_Glyph

Expect a reply within three business days. Writing from the app, under Get support in Settings, fills in the address for you.

Frequently asked questions

The Start button is greyed out.

Glyph mode needs your own Glyph list file (glyph_line.csv) before it can start. Add it from the card on the Home screen, or in Settings, once. Counting gear needs no file: switch Glyph assistance off in Settings.

Nothing shows in the Dynamic Island.

Live Activities have to be on for the app: open the iPhone's Settings app, find Dynamic Glyph, and turn them on. On iOS 18 to 26 the Island cannot be used on its own — the floating window has to stay open for the app to keep reading.

The floating window is in the way.

Pinch it to resize, drag it to move it, or tuck it against the side of the screen. Keep it clear of the slot row at the top of the game: that row is where the app reads the sequence from.

Does it work without Ingress?

No. Live recognition reads what the game draws on your screen, so Ingress has to be installed and running. Dynamic Glyph does not communicate with the game or a developer-operated server. Optional private iCloud sync is separate; it is off by default. You can review saved results without the game running.

Where are my results, and how do I delete them?

In Statistics, on the phone. The app keeps up to 1,000 hacks and 1,000 drops for 90 days. Clear results, in the result options, deletes them; single rows can be deleted from Select mode; Export writes a CSV you can keep.

The item counts look wrong.

Gear is read from the panels the game shows after a hack, and a panel that is covered or scrolled past can be missed. Open that drop and tap Report this reading… to create a report with the reading and its picture. You choose whether and how to share it; nothing is sent automatically.

What you need

  • An iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
  • Ingress, installed and signed in. It is a separate app and is not included.
  • For Glyph assistance, your own Glyph list file; for predictions, your own sequence list too. For counting gear, nothing extra.
  • For the Dynamic Island display, an iPhone that has one, with Live Activities on.
